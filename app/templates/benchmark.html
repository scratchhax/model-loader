This takes over the machine for about .

  • models, prompts × repetitions = requests.
  • Every loaded model gets unloaded. OpenWebUI requests will stall or fail for the whole run, including anything mid-conversation.
  • Models are loaded and evicted one at a time — this backend holds only one.
  • Nothing is written to models.ini. Models run with the settings they already have.
  • You can stop at any time; results collected so far are kept.
