{# LIVE MOTION for the overview. The panels are server-rendered and swapped by HTMX every second (every half second for the hero), which on its own moves things in steps: a chart redrawn by the server shifts left once per poll, and an eased transition that finishes before the next poll leaves the screen still for the rest of the interval - move, pause, move. The rule here is the owner's: if readings arrive every second, a change takes one second, at a steady speed, so motion from one reading runs straight into the next. * Time charts are drawn in the browser, every frame, from /api/v1/series (polled each second). Their element is hx-preserve, so the panel swap around it leaves it alone. Each line ends at "now"; its leading edge travels to every newest reading over one interval. * Numbers marked data-count count from the old value to the new one over their panel's poll interval, hooked on HTMX's swap. * Rings and bars move through CSS transitions whose duration is their panel's poll interval, linear (in the templates, not here). Colour: a tenant's KIND colour says who; the HEAT ramp (slate, indigo, violet, orange) says how hard something works against its ceiling. Charts pinned to a ceiling draw in heat. The same ramp is app.web.heat() on the server, for the ring arcs. The frame loop also runs off a timer when animation frames stall, which some kiosk browsers do to pages they think are in the background; a frozen chart is worse than a slower one. #}
