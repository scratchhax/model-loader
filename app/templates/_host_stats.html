Sits directly under the power row and shares its dark instrument styling, because the two are the same kind of reading - facts about the machine rather than about a model - and splitting them visually would imply they are not. Each card shows the number a DECISION is made on, which is not always the largest one available. Memory shows what is available, not what is used, because the only question ever asked of it here is "will the next model fit". Disk shows free for the same reason: weights are 18-120 GB apiece, so a used-percentage that looks comfortable can still mean the next download fails. Swap gets a card even though this box has none, and that is the point. With no swap there is no slack: a model sized a few GB past available RAM is an OOM kill rather than a slow run. A row that omitted swap would let a reader assume a safety net that is not there. Bars are drawn against the resource's own total, so the filled fraction is literally the fraction consumed.
