A strip is always in view - the page keeps it pinned under the header - and costs one line. Each reading is the number a decision is made on (free RAM, free disk, watts against the limit), with a mark beside it: a mini curve for the two that move second to second and whose shape matters (power, heat), a ring for the four that are a share of a whole.
