Each panel now answers, top to bottom: how busy and how full (ring gauge: util inside, VRAM by tenant outside) who is working on this card right now (the headline, and the panel's glow) who has been using it (one sparkline per tenant, 15 minutes) what each tenant is doing, in its own terms (rows: tok/s for a model, renders for ComfyUI) Colour follows the tenant's KIND (chat model, image, audio, other) and every row, segment and line names itself, so identity never rests on colour alone.
